Weapon Forge

The weapons the game draws, from the same src/utils/weaponShapes.js the combat stage renders. Each is authored in its own local frame (origin at the hand, blade along +X) so it can be drawn as a real profile: taper, point, bevel, edge glint, guard, bound grip, pommel. A shortbow is a longbow at half size and a dagger is a sword at half size — one profile per family, not two that can drift.
