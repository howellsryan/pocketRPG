What changed, and why.

Original PocketRPG geometry, informed by OSRS poses and gameplay.

Characters and contact

Thieving now has twelve distinct targets. The elf has pointed ears, long hair and a layered tunic; farmers have work clothes and hats; knights have closed helmets and plate; the Vyre has a pale face and formal clothing; the volcanic merchant has a separate stone body. Each pickpocket attempt follows a visible hip pouch, withdraws, then rests. Stall theft reaches for the cake on the counter.

References: Jagex’s elf model study, pickpocketable elf in gameplay, knight pickpocket pose, master farmer variants.

Work that reads as the skill

The approved Mining lift, hold and strike are preserved. Normal axes now use one working hand. Small-net fishing uses a hand-held mesh net, cage fishing stays more upright, rods cast and wait, and harpoons have a separate thrust. Herb gathering reaches from a standing stance. Saplings use a pot and young tree; fruit picking reaches the branch and brings the fruit down. Agility separates a balance beam from rooftop gaps.

References: Mining frames, axe work, fishing gameplay, herb harvesting frames.

Crafting and Fletching now hold their work: a chisel meets a gem, a knife works a log, sewing pulls a thread from supported leather, stringing stretches the bow string, and arrow assembly brings the component to the shaft. Potion mixing uses two vials with a short pour. Furnace work reaches into the furnace. Smithing and Construction keep distinct hammer targets.

OSRS’s separate method clips are documented in RuneLite’s animation definitions. These support the method distinctions; they do not supply PocketRPG’s geometry or exact keyframes.

Casting and ritual

Alchemy cups the hands around a gold pulse. Superheat raises one hand with a warm burst. Plank Make and magical tanning lift their material overhead. Enchanting uses hand-held work. Altar staging is reserved for actual altar rituals. Burying bends from the hips and resets; firemaking scrapes at an unlit log before ignition.

Inspected animation references: Alchemy, Superheat, Plank Make, Tan Leather, burying, firemaking.

Adaptation and review

Summoning, Dungeoneering, physical tanning and the game’s human or undead hunts have no direct OSRS equivalent. They use original motions in the same restrained, faceted style. The existing recipes, action durations and awarded items are unchanged. Related recipes share a working method and use their actual item colours.

The gallery covers all 339 recurring training actions and 58 planting/harvesting operations. New frames have four times the pixels; closer framing makes the hands and characters easier to inspect. Use normal playback, half speed, the frame slider and the 200 × 128 panel. The previous Thieving artwork is shown beside that panel for comparison.

The review gate checks fixed limbs, planted feet where appropriate, tool and forearm clearance against the player’s head and torso, working-tip contact, pocket contact, reduced motion, pause, scrolling and real completion handling. Those checks complement the visual review; they are not a claim that every mesh pair is collision-free or that these are exact OSRS replicas.
