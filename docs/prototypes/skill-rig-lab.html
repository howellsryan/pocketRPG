Tick-locked

Swing timing divides the action's real tick count, and the last strike lands exactly as the bar reaches 100%. That impact is what starts the payoff — nothing free-runs.

The payoff

The rock bursts into shards, the tree creaks then falls and settles, the rod snaps up and the catch is taken out of the air by hand. Then everything resets for the next action.

Ink flips, figure doesn't

The outline takes its colour from the ground — dark ink on parchment, warm bone on iron. Same geometry, same rig, two semantic tokens.

Cost

Around 70 SVG primitives and one transform per part per frame. No sprite sheets, no WebGL, no new dependency — it inlines into the single-file build.
