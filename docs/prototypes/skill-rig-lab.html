Tint ladder

Tick-locked

Every action swings at the same tempo. A longer one takes more strikes, not slower ones — runeforged ore is 23 swings, tin is 3. The last one lands exactly as the bar reaches 100%.

The payoff

The rock bursts into shards. The trunk lets go at the notch the axe has been cutting, topples and drops, leaving a stump. The rod snaps up and the catch is taken out of the air by hand.

Ink flips, figure doesn't

The outline takes its colour from the ground — dark ink on parchment, warm bone on iron. Same geometry, same rig, two semantic tokens.

Cost

Around 70 SVG primitives and one transform per part per frame, plus 8.6 KB of fish art lifted straight from bespokeIcons.json. No sprite sheets, no WebGL, no new dependency.
