# Brimhaven

PocketRPG analogue: **Brimhollow** &middot; 707 characters

```
Top-down fantasy tropical port map, painted tabletop-RPG battle-map style on aged parchment. A humid jungle harbour town with a busy stone quay, docked galleons, and stacked cargo crates. Thatched and tiled buildings cluster along the waterfront, palm trees swaying between them. A dim gambling den with flickering lantern light sits near the docks, and a fenced agility arena of rope bridges lies at the jungle's edge. A distant volcanic peak vents smoke over the dense green jungle canopy behind the town. Background fades to blank aged parchment with warm tropical vignette staining at the corners. Orthographic top-down view, warm tropical greens and teal water, hazy volcanic light. No text, no labels.
```
